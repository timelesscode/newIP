RESONANCE HARVEST

A Manga in Three Arcs

ARC 1: THE SCRAP-DRIFTER

---

Chapter 1: "Static"

PAGE 1 — FULL SPREAD: THE GRAVEYARD

A massive, silent debris field stretches across black space. Thousands of ship carcasses drift—capital ships cracked open, fighters twisted into metal knots, all frozen in slow rotation. Some still spark faintly.

CAPTION: The Verge Nebula. A thousand-year war ended here. Now it's just… salvage.

SMALL PANEL: A hand reaches out, fingers spread against a cockpit window. The reflection shows a young woman's face—KIVA, 19, sharp eyes, a faded pilot patch on her sleeve reading "421st Scavenger Wing."

KIVA (thought): Six days since last haul. Six days.

PAGE 2

Her ship, the EMBER, is a patchwork machine. Different-colored hull plates welded together. A Resonator dish on the nose, cracked but functional. The Harvester Claw—a massive mechanical arm bolted to the underside—looks like it was ripped off a mining rig.

She pilots through the graveyard, sensors pinging.

SFX: PING… PING…

KIVA: "Come on. One active core. That's all I need."

PAGE 3

Her screen flashes. A waveform appears—jagged, aggressive, pulsing red.

KIVA: "That's not debris."

The debris around her moves. Three ships unfold from hiding positions—wraith-like, angular, painted with the symbol of a broken crown. CROWN REAVERS.

RADIO (crackling): "Scavenger. Drop your cargo and we let you drift."

Kiva's hand moves to the Resonator controls. Three dials. Three frequencies.

KIVA (thought): Reavers. They don't take prisoners.

PAGE 4 — ACTION SEQUENCE

She slams the throttle. The Ember lurches forward as laser fire shreds the space where she was.

SFX: BRRRRRZT—

She cycles frequencies. Red. Blue. Green. Red again. Her shields flicker—wrong match, wrong match—

KIVA: "Come on, come on—"

One Reaver drops in front of her. She sees its waveform: steady blue pulse.

She locks Blue.

PAGE 5 — FULL PAGE

The Reaver's lasers hit her shields—and dissolve. Blue energy ripples outward, harmless.

The Reaver pilot's visible surprise through his cockpit.

Kiva holds the frequency. Her ship shudders. The Resonator whines higher and higher—

SFX: WHUUUUUUM—

KIVA: "HARMONIZING."

PAGE 6

Her ship PHASE-DIVES—a streak of light that passes through the Reaver's shields. She emerges inside his bubble, so close she can see the sweat on his forehead.

REAVER PILOT: "WHAT—"

KIVA: "Harvester."

The mechanical claw on her ship's belly SNAPS forward. It punches through his cockpit glass—not killing him, but latching onto the Engine Vane behind his seat.

SFX: KRUNCH—RIIIIIP—

The claw retracts, dragging the smoking component with it.

The Reaver ship goes dark. Engines dead. Drifting.

KIVA: "Thanks for the boost."

PAGE 7

The other two Reavers hesitate. Kiva's display shows she has 4 seconds before ejection. She uses 2 to target the second Reaver's Weapon Capacitor.

The claw fires again—rips it free.

SFX: SNAP—

She's ejected. Her ship spins, but her engines flare with the new Engine Vane harvested—a burst of speed that sends her rocketing past the remaining Reaver.

KIVA: "One left. You want to drift, or fight?"

The last Reaver boosts away. The Ember drifts through the scattering debris.

PAGE 8

Kiva exhales. Her hands are shaking. She looks at her haul: 1 Engine Vane, 1 Weapon Capacitor.

KIVA: "Scrap Forge, here I come."

---

Chapter 2: "The Forge"

PAGE 9

A hollowed-out asteroid. Inside, a makeshift station cobbled from wrecked ships. THE SCRAP FORGE. Lanterns flicker. Scrappers trade components in shadows.

Kiva lands the Ember in a crowded bay. A massive figure waits—VARIK, a scarred older man with one mechanical arm that's just another Harvester Claw.

VARIK: "Reaver parts. You're getting bold, kid."

KIVA: "They jumped me. I jumped back."

She tosses him the Engine Vane.

VARIK: "Three more of these, I can tune your Resonator. Two frequencies at once."

KIVA (grinning): "Now you're talking."

PAGE 10

A commotion across the bay. A ship has landed—sleek, military-grade, marked with SOLAR COMMAND insignia. Two figures exit.

The first: COMMANDER REN, a cold-eyed woman in an admiral's coat, her left arm replaced with a sleek prosthetic that hums faintly.

The second: CAELAN, a young man in pilot gear, maybe 22, with a Resonator on his wrist—a personal unit, not ship-mounted. His face is guarded.

VARIK (low): "Command doesn't come here unless they want something."

PAGE 11

Ren's eyes sweep the Forge. They land on Kiva.

REN: "You. The one with the Ember. You survived a Crown Reaver ambush yesterday. Solo."

KIVA: "News travels fast."

REN: "I have a job. The Reavers are massing at the Nexus Gate. An old Resonator core. If they activate it—"

KIVA: "Not my war."

REN: "They'll sweep every scrapper in the Verge. Including you."

Ren places a datachip on the table. It pulses with a gold waveform.

REN: "That's the Nexus core's frequency. Match it, and you can phase into the heart of their fleet. Disable it from inside. I'll have a fleet waiting."

KIVA: "Why me?"

REN: "Because my pilots know how to fight. You know how to harvest. "

PAGE 12

Kiva looks at Varik. He shakes his head slightly.

CAELAN (stepping forward): "I'll be on your wing. Two Resonators are better than one."

His eyes meet hers. There's something there—not arrogance, but a weight. Like he's done this before and lost something.

KIVA: "What's your frequency, pretty boy?"

CAELAN: "Multiphase. I can match three colors at once. I just can't… harvest."

KIVA: "So you're the shield, I'm the claw."

CAELAN: "Something like that."

PAGE 13

She takes the chip.

KIVA: "If I do this, I keep everything I harvest. Every component. Every core."

REN: "Done."

As Ren walks away, Caelan lingers.

CAELAN (quiet): "She's lying about the fleet. There is no fleet. It's just us."

KIVA: "Then why are you coming?"

CAELAN: He pulls back his sleeve. His arm is scarred with Resonator burns—circuit-like patterns etched into his skin.

CAELAN: "Because the Reavers have my brother. And if they activate that gate… I'll never get him back."

ARC 2: THE NEXUS RUN

Chapter 3: "Harmonic Convergence"

PAGE 14 — DOUBLE SPREAD

The Nexus Gate. A massive ring of ancient metal, covered in Resonator crystals the size of buildings. Around it, dozens of Reaver ships form a defensive sphere. In the center, a flagship bigger than anything Kiva's ever seen.

The Ember and Caelan's ship—the LANCE—approach from the debris field, engines cold, drifting silent.

CAELAN (radio): "They've got a perimeter. Three layers. First layer, red frequency. Second, blue. Third, green."

KIVA: "They're rotating them. Like a lock."

CAELAN: "We have to hit the gap. When the layers mismatch—"

KIVA: "I know how a frequency lock works."

CAELAN: "Then you know if we're off by half a second, we get shredded."

PAGE 15

They wait. Kiva's hand hovers over her Resonator dials. Sweat on her brow.

On the Lance, Caelan closes his eyes. His wrist-mounted Resonator glows. His scarred arm pulses in rhythm.

CAELAN: "Now."

They fire engines. The ships scream toward the Reaver perimeter.

PAGE 16-17 — ACTION SPREAD

The first layer—red. Caelan matches it, his shield bubble expanding to cover both ships. Red lasers dissolve on contact.

Second layer—blue. He switches. Seamless. Kiva watches him work in awe.

Third layer—green. They're through.

But alarms blare. Reaver fighters scramble.

KIVA: "I'm going in. Cover me."

She breaks formation, diving toward the flagship. Caelan's shields stretch, barely keeping the swarm off her tail.

PAGE 18

Kiva locks onto the flagship's waveform. It's not a simple color—it's a complex pattern, pulsing gold, silver, black. The Nexus core's frequency.

KIVA: "That's… that's three frequencies at once. Layered."

She can't match it. Not alone.

RADIO: "Kiva!"

Caelan's ship is taking hits. His shields flicker.

CAELAN: "I can match it. But I'll be exposed. No shields. If you don't harvest that core in five seconds—"

KIVA: "You'll be Reaver scrap."

CAELAN: "Yeah."

PAGE 19

She looks at the flagship. At her cracked Resonator dish. At Varik's words: Three more and I can tune it.

She doesn't have three. She has one.

KIVA: "Do it."

Caelan's Resonator flares—gold, silver, black all at once. His ship goes dark as all power routes to the harmonization. His shields drop.

Reaver fire tears toward him.

PAGE 20 — FULL PAGE

Kiva's dish cracks further—but she locks onto his frequency. Uses it as a bridge. Her own harmonization builds, faster than ever—

SFX: WHUUUUUUM—

She phase-dives through the flagship's hull. Inside the core chamber.

The Nexus core floats before her. A pulsating crystal the size of a person. Its waveform is beautiful. Terrifying.

KIVA: Five seconds.

She fires the Harvester Claw.

Chapter 4: "What We Harvest"

PAGE 21

The claw latches onto the core. But it doesn't rip free—it connects. Kiva's vision floods with light.

VISION SEQUENCE (2-3 pages):

She sees the Reaver leader—THE CROWN, a figure in a broken crown, sitting in a throne of harvested components. He's old. Ancient. His body is more machine than flesh.

THE CROWN: "The Nexus doesn't destroy. It remembers. Every frequency, every soul harvested in the war… it's all in here."

Kiva sees Caelan's brother—DORIAN, younger, wild-eyed, strapped to a Resonator harness, forced to feed his frequency into the core.

THE CROWN: "I'm not activating a weapon. I'm opening a door. To the other side. To the dead. Don't you want to see them again?"

Kiva's mother appears. A pilot, her ship exploding. Kiva reaching out—too far—

KIVA: "No."

She yanks the claw.

PAGE 24

The core rips free. The flagship goes dark. Alarms scream.

Kiva is ejected—her ship spinning through the chaos. The Nexus Gate begins to collapse, the core's frequency destabilizing.

PAGE 25

She sees Caelan's Lance, drifting. He took hits protecting her. His cockpit is cracked. He's unconscious.

Reaver fighters are regrouping. They want the core.

KIVA: Not today.

She slams the harvested core into her own Resonator. The Ember SCREAMS with power. Her ship transforms—shards of golden light extending like wings.

PAGE 26-27 — DOUBLE SPREAD

She doesn't phase-dive. She phase-slices. Flying through Reaver ships like a blade, the core's frequency severing their components. Engines rip free. Weapons arrays fall. Hull plates scatter.

She's not destroying them. She's harvesting them all at once.

In thirty seconds, the Reaver fleet is drifting. Disarmed. Helpless.

Kiva lands on the Lance's hull, prying open Caelan's cockpit with her bare hands (the core's energy coursing through her).

KIVA: "Wake up. I found your brother."

ARC 3: THE FREQUENCY OF PEACE

Chapter 5: "Scars"

PAGE 28

Three days later. The Scrap Forge.

Dorian sits in a makeshift medical bay, the Resonator harness removed but scars remaining. Caelan sits beside him, not speaking. Just present.

VARIK (to Kiva): "Command wants the core. Ren's been asking."

KIVA: "She can ask."

She looks at the Nexus core, now housed in a containment field. It pulses gently. Calm.

KIVA: "This thing has a thousand years of war inside it. Frequencies of people who died thinking they were fighting for something."

VARIK: "And?"

KIVA: "I think we should let them go."

PAGE 29

She approaches the core. Her hand touches the containment field.

CAELAN: "Kiva. If you release it—"

KIVA: "Then the war's really over. No more Resonator weapons. No more harvesting pilots. Just… salvage."

She looks at him.

KIVA: "Your brother is alive. That's enough."

PAGE 30 — FULL PAGE

She releases the containment. The core's frequency expands—not a weapon, but a wave. It passes through the Forge. Through the Verge. Through every ship, every station, every debris field.

Where it passes, the dead frequencies—the echoes of a thousand destroyed ships—finally fade.

The war ends. Not with a victory. With a silence.

PAGE 31

Kiva stands at the edge of the Forge, looking out at the graveyard. Caelan joins her.

CAELAN: "You just gave up the most powerful weapon in the sector."

KIVA: "I harvest components, Caelan. I don't collect ghosts."

A beat.

CAELAN: "What now?"

KIVA: "There's always more salvage."

She grins.

KIVA: "And I heard the Eastern Reaches have a new kind of waveform. Something nobody's ever seen."

CAELAN: "You want me to come?"

She tosses him a Resonator—a new one, built from harvested Reaver parts.

KIVA: "Someone's gotta watch my back while I harvest."

FINAL PAGE — FULL SPREAD

The Ember and the Lance fly side by side, away from the Forge, toward a distant nebula. Behind them, the graveyard slowly rotates—no longer a memorial, just debris.

Waiting to be harvested.

CAPTION: In space, nothing ends. It just… changes frequency.

CHARACTER PROFILES

KIVA — "The Scavenger"

· Age: 19
· Ship: Ember (patchwork fighter)
· Resonator: Single-frequency, cracked but reliable
· Harvester: Mechanical claw, salvaged from mining rig
· Drive: Survival, then purpose. Lost her mother in the war. Doesn't believe in causes anymore—until she finds one worth believing in.
· Fighting style: Precise, opportunistic. She doesn't win fights—she dismantles them.

CAELAN — "The Resonant"

· Age: 22
· Ship: Lance (military-grade interceptor)
· Resonator: Multiphase (wrist-mounted, burns his arm with use)
· Harvester: None—he's pure defense
· Drive: Guilt. He left his brother Dorian behind during a Reaver ambush. He's been chasing him ever since.
· Fighting style: Disciplined, self-sacrificing. He'll take any hit to protect his wingman.

VARIK — "The Forge Master"

· Age: Unknown (50s? 60s? Hard to tell with the scars)
· Role: Scrap Forge operator, mentor figure
· Philosophy: "Components have frequencies too. Treat them right, they'll treat you right."
· Secret: He was a Resonator engineer during the war. Designed the first multiphase systems. He's the one who knows the core's true nature.

COMMANDER REN — "The Prosthetic"

· Age: 40s
· Role: Solar Command officer
· Resonator: Integrated into her prosthetic arm
· Goal: Control. She sees the Nexus core as the ultimate weapon. She's not evil—she's just been fighting so long she doesn't know how to stop.

THE CROWN — "The Reaver King"

· Age: Ancient
· Role: Antagonist, but not a villain
· Truth: He was the first Resonator pilot. He's been trying to open the Nexus Gate for centuries—not to conquer, but to see the dead again. His wife. His squadron. Everyone he lost. He's a man who couldn't let go.

VOLUME 2 TEASE

A new frequency appears in the Eastern Reaches. Not red, blue, or green. Something else. Something that doesn't just harmonize—it changes. Ships that encounter it come back… wrong. Their pilots speaking languages that don't exist. Frequencies that shouldn't be possible.

Kiva and Caelan go to investigate.

They find a ship drifting. Inside, a single survivor. A girl with no scars, no pilot patch, no name. But when she opens her mouth, her voice carries a frequency that makes every Resonator in the sector scream.

Her name is ECHO. And she's not from this war.

She's from the next one.
